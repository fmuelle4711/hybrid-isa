======================================================================================================================
WHY ALL THREE FUSIONS ARE EXACT
======================================================================================================================
  A multitone drive applies its tones SIMULTANEOUSLY, so the Hamiltonian
  during the pulse is their SUM and the block realises exp[-i(sum)t].
  H_lab carries each of these groups as a sum:
      QT      J_AB0 X_AB + J_AC0 X_AC
      QT-SB   g_abl X_AB x_l + g_acl X_AC x_l
      QPS     the three level phases
  so the summed exponential IS that group's evolution.  These are the
  generalized (higher-rank) generators of Table I, driven directly.

  THE SEQUENTIAL PRODUCT IS THE APPROXIMATION, not the multitone:
      max|multitone - sequential|,  QT group      2.53e-05
      max|multitone - sequential|,  QT-SB centre  1.97e-05
  Splitting a group into consecutive single-tone gates introduces that
  error; driving it as one block does not.

  This corrects two labels this table previously carried.  QT_multi was
  marked SYNTHESIZED and QT-SB_multi MEASURED, both on the premise that
  the multitone block had to reproduce the sequential product.  It does
  not: the sequential product was never the target.  The JSON's
  centre_operator_check numbers remain valid, but they measure how much
  error the SEQUENTIAL split would have introduced, not an error in the
  merge:
      max_commutator_bare          1.0
      max_commutator_tau_scaled    3.94875e-05
      max_abs_diff                 1.9743590054108117e-05
      infidelity                   5.247491330351295e-11

  Non-commutation is why the split is lossy.  It is not an obstacle to
  the multitone block, which never performs the split.
