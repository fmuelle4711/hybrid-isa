====================================================================================================
HOW THIS GIVES 32
====================================================================================================
  Per HALF-step, counted as logical operations:
      H_0                4 Rotation + 2 RZ            =  6
      H_1                3 CR + 3 CD                  =  6
      H_2XX + H_2YY      2 exchange J + 1 CD(link)    =  3
                                                        --
                                                        15
  The two link-modulated exchange terms sit ONCE at the centre of the
  symmetric step at full tau, not in either half:
      2 x 15 + 2 = 32

  TWO PLACES THIS IS EASY TO GET WRONG.
    1. Exchange J is ONE logical operation per pair, even though
       H_2XX and H_2YY are separate Trotter pieces in the code.  The
       XX/YY split is a PULSE-level fact -- a bichromatic MS window
       gives XX or YY, not their sum -- and is exact because
       [XX, YY] = 0.  It is what takes 4 exchange operations to 8 MS
       windows in Appendix B, and it does NOT change the 32.
    2. CD appears in two pieces: three local ones in H_1 and the
       link-mode one in H_2XX, giving 4 per half-step and 8 per step,
       which is the Conditional displacement row of tab:qubit32.

  This reproduces the count in Table tab:qubit32 and the logical
  column of tab:gatecounts.  Verified independently against the drawn
  tape by scripts/paper_gate_numbers.py, which reads 32 from the
  circuit rather than from this arithmetic.

====================================================================================================
CROSS-CHECK AGAINST THE LEVEL-ENCODED BUILDER
====================================================================================================
  simulations/circuit_dissipative_kraus.py::build_sub_hamiltonians('B')
  builds the SAME Hamiltonian grouped six ways instead of four, using
  projectors rather than Paulis.  The two agree because
      Pi_R = (I - sigma_z^R)/2   on the single-excitation code space,
  so each sigma_z term there appears as an identity piece plus a
  projector piece, e.g.
      -chi_a/2 * I (x) n_a  +  chi_a * Pi_A (x) n_a
  The identity pieces are unconditional mode rotations and are
  absorbed into the free frame; the projector pieces are the CR and CD
  gates above.  Same coefficient names, same values.

  The four-piece split is the one to quote in Appendix B: it is what
  the executed three-qubit circuit actually applies, and its ordering
  is the one the 32 count is derived from.
