  CAVEATS
    * QT is flagged entangling=True in vibronic_scaling.py but acts on a
      SINGLE wire -- it is an intra-qudit level transition and entangles
      nothing.  Counted here as non-entangling on wire count; the
      builder's flag disagrees and is noted above.
    * CNOT is counted as ONE entangling primitive.  On a trapped ion it
      is itself an MS plus local rotations, and no CNOT duration is
      anchored in this repo, so the qubit-native entangling count is a
      LOWER BOUND.
    * The qudit 28-gate builder fuses QPS and QT.  QPS fusion is exact
      (commuting projectors); QT fusion is NOT exact by commutation
      ([|A><B|,|A><C|] = 1.0, measured) and rests on a multi-tone
      hardware claim priced from step_qt_meth_aggcap.py.
    * The 28-gate qudit builder is NOT the same builder as
      build_qudit_trotter_step (which gives 36 unfused / 34 QPS-fused).
      Comparing 28 against the qubit numbers mixes builders; stated here,
      not resolved.
