1  LOGICAL HISTOGRAM BY GATE TYPE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
        gate  count        formula   what it is                                  
         chi      6            2Nc   sigma_z (x) n, per-site dispersive (CR)     
          cd      8          4Nc-4   sigma_z (x) x, per-site + link (CD)         
    Rotation      8          4Nc-4   mode free evolution                         
          rz      4        2(Nc-1)   qubit detuning, virtual-Z                   
           J      4        2(Nc-1)   2-qubit XY exchange (XX+YY)/2               
          Jl      2        2(Nc-2)   2-qubit exchange (x) x_l, 3-body            
       TOTAL     32        16Nc-16
  Rotation and cd are counted 4Nc-4, audited against Vu Eq. 18 to
  21: one mode free evolution per mode per half step over the
  2Nc-2 modes, and per site one conditional displacement on its
  high mode and one on its low mode, boundary low modes dropped.
  J_l is counted 2(Nc-2), the chain link count of Vu Eq. 21 with
  Eq. 52's boundary rule, not Nc-1.  The two agree at Nc = 3, where
  both bonds are end bonds, so every number in this table is
  unchanged; the closed form differs for Nc > 3.

3  WHERE THE +26 COMES FROM
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  source                                           timed   delta vs logical
  CR + CD + Rotation, 1:1 with logical                22                 +0
  rz -> virtual-Z, no pulse                            0                 -4
  J -> 2 MS windows each                               8                 +4
  Jl -> XX sandwich + YY sandwich                     28                +26
  TOTAL                                               58                +26

  So essentially ALL the inflation is Jl (+26 of +26 net, before the
  -4 from rz becoming free and the +4 from J doubling).  26 of the 32
  logical operations are 1:1 with a native pulse; the two Jl gates carry
  the entire compilation cost.

4  THE Jl SANDWICH, GATE BY GATE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  per Jl coupling, XX half:
      H      1-wire  timed
      H      1-wire  timed
      CNOT   2-wire  timed
      CD     2-wire  timed
      CNOT   2-wire  timed
      H      1-wire  timed
      H      1-wire  timed
  per Jl coupling, YY half (RZ(+-pi/2) conjugation rotates XX -> YY):
      Z      1-wire  virtual
      H      1-wire  timed
      Z      1-wire  virtual
      H      1-wire  timed
      CNOT   2-wire  timed
      CD     2-wire  timed
      CNOT   2-wire  timed
      H      1-wire  timed
      Z      1-wire  virtual
      H      1-wire  timed
      Z      1-wire  virtual

  per Jl: 2x CD (timed), 4x CNOT (timed), 8x H (timed), 4x Z (virtual)
  x 2 couplings = 4x CD, 8x CNOT, 16x H, 8x Z

  ONE sandwich alone realises XX only, not XX+YY -- measured +1.000*XX
  by h_basis_check (scripts/circuit_qubit_hybridlane.py).  Both halves
  are required, and the split is exact because [XX(x)x, YY(x)x] = 0.

6  CNOT PRICING -- WHY 58 IS A LOWER BOUND
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  8 CNOTs appear, each counted as ONE timed primitive.
  On a trapped ion a CNOT is itself a Molmer-Sorensen plus local
  rotations, and NO CNOT duration is anchored anywhere in this repo
  (searched; only gate-set mentions in docs/scaling_theorems.md).
  So 58 is a LOWER BOUND on the timed-pulse count.

  The runtime table does NOT price the sandwich at all: it holds H_Jl at
  a single MS window (200 us).  Pricing it from the decomposition instead
  gave 412.57 us per Jl -- 2.06x MORE -- but that rests on an ASSUMED
  CNOT time, so it was printed and NOT adopted (runtime_table.md).
  Holding Jl at one window is therefore the conservative choice AGAINST
  the qudit advantage, not for it.
