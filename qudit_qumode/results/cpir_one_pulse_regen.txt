========================================================================================================
WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
========================================================================================================
  Dropping the echo is a physics claim, not a counting one.  This
  script only reprices; it does not show the uncompensated gate
  works.  The supporting evidence already in the repo is:
    results/qcr_mechanism_paper_angle.txt   single segment, VZ absorbed: 1-F = 2.900031e-03, leak 1.840207e-03
    results/qcr_echo_physical.txt           full echo,      VZ absorbed: 1-F = 3.918260e-03, leak 3.151496e-03
  i.e. the echo is WORSE in this closed-system replay.  The argument
  against dropping it is experimental: a virtual Z_d has to know the
  light shift, which drifts with laser intensity, while the echo
  cancels it without knowing it.  That trade must be stated in the
  paper if the one-pulse convention is adopted.

  NO ADOPTION.  Report only.
