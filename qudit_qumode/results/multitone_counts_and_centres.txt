  WHAT THE FUSIONS COST IN EXACTNESS -- unchanged by this read:
    QPS fusion   EXACT.   All QPS generators are diagonal projectors on
                 one wire; measured [Pi_A, Pi_B] = 0.000000.
    QT fusion    NOT exact.  |A><B| and |A><C| share level A;
                 measured commutator 1.000000.  Rests on a multitone
                 hardware claim, not an operator identity.

                 qutip reference trotter_method_a.py:212 actually uses,
                 so it moves the circuit TOWARD its own reference.
                 At the circuit angle the merged and sequential centres

                 (results/circuit_qudit_27.txt).

  the whole op-count gap.  NO ADOPTION -- a count, not a repricing.

========================================================================================================
2  CENTRE ORDERING IN THE TABLE III SHOT CIRCUITS
========================================================================================================
  Question: were the central J_l (qubit) / QT-SB (qudit) terms applied
  as ONE SUMMED exponential, or SEQUENTIALLY?

  First, a correction to the premise: 'hqml stinespring' describes the
  N=8 row only.  Per results/table3_provenance.txt the backends are
      N=4  classical_kraus_shots            (both encodings)
      N=8  hqml_qnode_stinespring_shots     (qudit)
           hqml_qnode_stinespring_b_qubit   (qubit)
  so the four cells are produced by THREE different code paths.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  N=4, BOTH ENCODINGS  ->  SUMMED
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  path: generate_figs_hqml_comparison.py maps both methods onto
        circuit_dissipative_kraus.build_sub_hamiltonians(builder, N),
        with _METHOD_TO_BUILDER = {a_qudit: 'A', a_qudit_d: 'A',
        b_qubit: 'B'}, then build_u_step.

  The centre is assembled as a SINGLE Hamiltonian and exponentiated once:
       106 |         H_QTSB = (P.J_AB0*P.eta_AB*(trans_d(_A,_B)+trans_d(_B,_A))*xld
       107 |                   + P.J_AC0*P.eta_AC*(trans_d(_A,_C)+trans_d(_C,_A))*xld)
      ...  (method 'B', the qubit column, is the same shape)
       121 |         H_QTSB = (P.g_abl*(trans_d(_A,_B)+trans_d(_B,_A))*xld
       122 |                   + P.g_acl*(trans_d(_A,_C)+trans_d(_C,_A))*xld)
  and applied as one exponential of that sum:
       202 |     Umid = U('QTSB', dt)
      -> H_QTSB is the SUM of the A-B and A-C terms, and Umid is ONE
         exp(-i H_QTSB dt).  SUMMED, for both the qubit and the qudit
         column at N=4.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  N=8, QUDIT  ->  SEQUENTIAL
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  path: circuit_fig89_10_hqml_qnode.py imports the unitary step from
        circuit_fig6_hqml.one_trotter_step and wraps it in Stinespring
        channels.  Its centre is TWO separate sideband gates:
       122 |     # --- H_QTSB centre, full tau (2 sideband) ---
       123 |     hqml.QT(P.J_AB0 * P.eta_AB * tau_full, level_i=_L_A, level_j=_L_B,
       124 |             wires=['q', 'l'], sideband=True)
       125 |     hqml.QT(P.J_AC0 * P.eta_AC * tau_full, level_i=_L_A, level_j=_L_C,
       126 |             wires=['q', 'l'], sideband=True)
      -> two hqml.QT(..., sideband=True) calls, each at full tau.
         SEQUENTIAL.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  N=8, QUBIT  ->  SEQUENTIAL
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  path: circuit_fig89_10_hqml_qnode_b_qubit.py, a literal 3-qubit
        circuit on wires QB[0..2] plus one ancilla.  Its step is
       221 |     h = tau_full / 2.0
       222 |     _H0(h)
       223 |     _H1(h)
       224 |     _H2XX(h)
       225 |     _H2YY(h)
       226 |     _H2YY(h, reverse=True)
       227 |     _H2XX(h, reverse=True)
       228 |     _H1(h,  reverse=True)
       229 |     _H0(h,  reverse=True)
      The J_l pieces are the g_alt displacement sandwiches inside
      _H2XX and _H2YY, emitted one pair at a time in a Python loop:
       190 |             val = g_alt[i - 1] * tau / 2.0
       191 |             r, phi = float(np.abs(val)), float(np.angle(val))
       192 |             hqml.ConditionalDisplacement(r, phi, wires=[ANC, MODES[3]])
       193 |             qml.SWAP(wires=[QB[0], ANC])
      -> the A-B and A-C link terms are separate gate sandwiches, and
         the XX and YY halves are separate blocks.  SEQUENTIAL on both
         counts; there is no single summed centre anywhere in this path.

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ANSWER
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  cell                   centre       generating path
  N=4  qubit             SUMMED       circuit_dissipative_kraus build_sub_hamiltonians('B') + build_u_step
  N=4  qudit             SUMMED       circuit_dissipative_kraus build_sub_hamiltonians('A') + build_u_step
  N=8  qubit             SEQUENTIAL   circuit_fig89_10_hqml_qnode_b_qubit one_trotter_step
  N=8  qudit             SEQUENTIAL   circuit_fig6_hqml one_trotter_step, via qnode.py

  CONSEQUENCE, reported not fixed.  The two rows of Table III now differ
  in THREE respects at once, not one:
      1. Fock cutoff        4       vs 8
      2. noise channel      classical Kraus vs Stinespring in-circuit
      3. centre ordering    summed  vs sequential
  Item 3 is new here; items 1 and 2 were recorded in
  results/table3_provenance.txt section 7.  Any reading of Table III as
  a clean Fock-convergence test is confounded by 2 and 3.

  Scale of item 3, from the measurements already in the repo: the
  summed-vs-sequential centre gap is 0.00377 % RMSE on the qudit
  (results/qtsb_summed_center.txt) and 0.00408 % on the qubit
  (results/jl_summed_center.txt).  Both are far below the ~1 % Table III
  entries, so this does not explain those numbers -- but it does mean
  the N=4 and N=8 rows are not the same circuit, and the paper should
  not describe them as one pipeline run at two cutoffs.

  A further note on the N=4 qubit column: builder 'B' is the cqed
  Hamiltonian expressed with trans_d/proj_d, i.e. in the QUDIT
  encoding, whereas the N=8 qubit column is a literal 3-qubit circuit.
  So 'qubit' does not mean the same object in the two rows either.
  Reported for the record; no number is changed.
