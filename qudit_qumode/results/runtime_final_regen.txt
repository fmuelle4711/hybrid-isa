  AUDITED CHAIN COUNTS.  Rotation and the R3 conditional
  displacement are both 4Nc-4: one mode free evolution per mode
  per half step over the 2Nc-2 modes, and per site one R3 on its
  high mode (Vu Eq. 20 term 2) plus one on its low mode (Eq. 21
  term 1) with the two boundary low modes dropped.  The qubit CD
  is split by rule, 4Nc-4 real ones at R3 and 4(Nc-2) zero-time
  ones inside the J_l; blending them diluted the mean by 12/8.
  Closed forms: qudit ISA 16Nc-14, qubit logical 16Nc-16,
  multitone 10Nc-3, MS windows 6Nc-8.  T0 is now identical on
  both sides, so T0_qubit - T0_qudit = 0 at every Nc.

  The link term C_Pi_x D, and the qubit J_l that
  mirrors it, are counted 2(Nc-2) per step, not Nc-1: Vu Eq. 21
  gives every bond two vibronic link terms, one per endpoint
  low-frequency mode, and Eq. 52 drops the two boundary modes, so
  the end bonds keep one each and interior bonds two.  The MS-window
  column is therefore 4(Nc-1) + 2(Nc-2) = 6Nc-8, giving 10/28/40/64.
  At Nc = 3 that is 10, unchanged, because both bonds are end bonds
  and 2(Nc-2) = Nc-1 = 2.  See results/cluster_bound.md section 5b.

  NOTE: leak and Omega_sb/delta are IDENTICAL at every detuning.
  That is exact in this model, not a coincidence.  With
  T = 2*pi/delta the required amplitude is
  Omega_sb = delta sqrt(2 theta/pi), so Omega_sb/delta = sqrt(2 theta/pi)
  and delta*T = 2*pi are both independent of delta, so in the
  simulated drive model the whole gate is the same evolution up to a
  rescaling of time.  Under this rule the detuning buys duration and
  costs laser power rather than trading against fidelity, which is
  what the published sweep column showed.  Fixed hardware
  frequencies absent from the model can break that.
