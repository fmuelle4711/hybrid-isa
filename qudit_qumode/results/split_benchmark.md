The qubit reference carries 5(N_c-1) windows per step and a split qudit 5 max(0, N_c - capacity). Once the window time dominates both, the ratio tends to (N_c-1)/(N_c-capacity), which approaches 1 as N_c grows at fixed capacity.

The same quantity is re-derived from the edge costs alone, with the cluster size K free, in `results/cluster_bound.md`, together with the bound and the (N_c-1)/(N_c-K) limit.
