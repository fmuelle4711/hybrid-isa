========================================================================================================
Q1  WHY QCR SHOWS 3.64e-06 HERE AND 2.9e-03 IN THE MECHANISM RERUN
========================================================================================================
  They propagate DIFFERENT Hamiltonians and answer different questions.

  3.64e-06  fig_pulses_sources.txt / this table
      Source: step_qcr_cr_two_points_v2_results.json, an IMPOSED-GENERATOR
      GRAPE run.  The control operator handed to the optimiser IS the gate
      generator, H_c = Pi_l (x) n, and the target is exp(-i theta Pi_l n).
      Replaying that waveform under that same operator therefore asks only:
      did the optimiser hit its own target?  With a single control that
      commutes with itself the answer is trivially yes -- the pulse is flat
      (std exactly 0) and the problem reduces to setting one integral.
      It contains NO physics about whether a laser can produce Pi_l (x) n.

  2.9e-03   results/qcr_mechanism_paper_angle.txt
      Source: the REAL off-resonant blue-sideband drive,
        H(t) = (Omega_sb/2)(|l><g| (x) a^dag e^{-i delta t} + h.c.),
      with NO imposed generator, compared against the effective generator
      that Schrieffer-Wolff predicts it should produce.  That is the
      physics question, and 2.9e-03 (endpoint, after VZ) is its answer.

  So the two numbers are not in conflict and neither is wrong.  3.64e-06
  is a compiler self-consistency check; 2.9e-03 is the gate's actual
  infidelity against the operator it is supposed to implement.  THE PAPER
  SHOULD QUOTE 2.9e-03 for QCR and must not present 3.64e-06 as a
  physical fidelity.  The same caveat applies to every imposed-generator
  row in this table -- see the note below.

========================================================================================================
  step_qcd_bazavan_full_lab_results.json   exists = True
  step_qcd_bazavan_full_lab_ramps_results.json   exists = True
  produced by  pulses_meth/hypulse_validation/step_qcd_bazavan_full_lab.py
               and step_qcd_bazavan_full_lab_ramps.py
  quoted in    pulses_meth/appendix_table.md, the table at lines 98-99

  That run takes the SAME final_v3 QCD waveform and propagates it with
  qutip.sesolve under the full time-dependent Bazavan Eq. 1 -- cos(delta t)
  carrier envelope and e^{+-i omega_z t} mode oscillations kept explicit,
  only the optical RWA applied -- rather than under the imposed
  sigma_z (x) X control operator.  Cutoff-converged over N in {8, 10, 12}.
  Reported there:
    unramped, T = 160.4 us   F = 0.999 / 0.999 / 0.998
    sin^2 ramps 5 us/side, T = 165.4 us   F = 0.999 / 0.999 / 0.999

  So QCD has BOTH kinds of number, and they differ for the same reason
  QCR's two numbers differ:
    imposed-generator replay (this table)  1-F = 1.130109e-05
    full Bazavan lab-frame replay          1-F ~ 1e-3 to 2e-3
  The Bazavan value is the physically meaningful one for QCD, and it is
  the analogue of the 2.9e-03 for QCR.

READ THIS TABLE WITH THE FOLLOWING CAVEAT
========================================================================================================
  QCD, CD, QT and QCR were all optimised against IMPOSED effective
  generators (the control operator is the gate generator).  Their
  common-protocol numbers are therefore self-consistency checks, not
  physical fidelities.  Only QT-SB is replayed here under its real
  time-dependent drive, because that is the model its waveform was
  optimised against.
